# Six-Form Human Construct Audit: Incomplete Cutoff

Evidence status: **post-primary audit (failed frozen eligibility gate; descriptive boundary evidence)**. The completed collection did not pass the frozen sample gate and cannot provide a fixed-rater construct endpoint.

## Eligibility Gate

The cutoff contains 31 complete submissions: 11 pass the frozen eligibility rules and 20 do not.
Valid selected submissions: 11/30. By form: A: 1/5 | B: 2/5 | C: 1/5 | D: 1/5 | E: 3/5 | F: 3/5.
Exclusions comprise assistance only=9, technical issue only=5, and both=6.
The retained responses provide 132 item labels. No item has the required five valid labels across all forms, and complete five-label PairAcc is unavailable.

## Data-Quality Diagnostics

Completion time ranges from 62 to 3734 seconds (median 330 seconds); 30 of 31 submissions finish below ten minutes versus the planned 12--15 minutes.
Eligible label-level referent agreement with author gold is 51/132 (38.6%).
Eligible label-level execution agreement is 34/132 (25.8%).
These unequal-form, incomplete-sample quantities are diagnostics only; they are not fixed-rater agreement or confirmatory endpoints.

## Post-Hoc Assistance-Relaxed Sensitivity

Ignoring the frozen assistance exclusion while retaining the technical-issue exclusion yields 20 participants (A: 2 | B: 4 | C: 2 | D: 4 | E: 4 | F: 4). Referent agreement is 92/240 (38.3%) and execution agreement is 61/240 (25.4%).
The questionnaire combines translation, generative AI, search, and other-person help in one response, so this slice cannot isolate benign translation and remains non-evidentiary.

## All-Primary Sensitivity

Using all 30 primary submissions despite frozen exclusions gives referent majority--gold 27/72, execution majority--gold 19/72, and changed-pair accuracy 3/18.
This complete-looking matrix is reported only to show that relaxing eligibility does not rescue the construct audit; it is not evidence.

## Interpretation and Follow-Up

The collection cannot strengthen the TRI construct claim. It instead documents a failed eligibility and engagement gate.
Additional eligibility-passing responses needed by form are A=4, B=3, C=4, D=4, E=2, F=2. Because the frozen allocation included only one reserve per form, any continuation beyond those slots requires a prospective protocol amendment before recruitment.
A clean follow-up should retain the frozen items and gold, separate translation from AI/search/other-person assistance, add a comprehension gate before formal items, and recruit from a monitored panel. No new model experiment repairs this human-evidence gap.
