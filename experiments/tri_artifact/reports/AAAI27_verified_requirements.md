## Length and evidence placement

- At most seven pages of main content.
- At most nine pages total.
- Pages after page seven are exclusively for references.
- Supplementary material is optional and reviewers are not required to review it.
- Evidence critical to evaluating the paper must appear in the main body.

The main paper should therefore use all seven permitted content pages efficiently. Extended
tables, prompts, task templates, complete traces, and secondary analyses may go in the technical
supplement, but the central definition, method, primary clustered statistics, unseen-domain
result, model-facing database result, limitations, and decisive related-work distinction must be
in the seven-page main body.

## Review and authorship notes

- Phase 1 uses two human reviews supplemented by one non-decisional AI-generated review.
- AAAI permits judicious use of generative AI in manuscript preparation, but authors remain
  fully responsible for correctness, attribution, references, and compliance.
- Non-existent references and plagiarism are explicitly sanctionable. All direct-neighbor
  references in this project must therefore be checked against primary sources before inclusion.
