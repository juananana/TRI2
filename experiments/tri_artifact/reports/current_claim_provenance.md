## Non-Evidence and Privacy Boundary

- `human_validation/annotation_key_private.csv`, raw participant workbooks, blank/coordination
  forms, and author trial files are excluded from the public artifact. De-identified normalized
  responses, aggregate reports, and analysis code are included.
- Internal author/red-team review reports are planning records, not empirical evidence, and are
  excluded from the submission artifact.
- API keys are neither source files nor artifact inputs; the archive builder scans for key-like
  byte patterns before writing the ZIP.
