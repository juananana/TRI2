# TRI Six-Form Human Construct Audit Protocol

## Status

- Evidence status before completed analysis: planned/unverified.
- Intended status after all frozen checks pass: post-primary human construct audit.
- The questionnaire, answer key, allocation, and exclusion rules were frozen before collection.
- This study annotates existing authored and rewritten instructions. It is not open-language evidence.

## Frozen sample

Six disjoint forms contain 12 items each. Five valid labels are required per item. The 72 items
contain 36 changed rows forming 18 complete Preserve/Reevaluate pairs, 12 Stable rows, 12
action-invalid rows, and 12 existing human rewrites.

Each form has five primary participant codes and one reserve code. Analysis uses the first completed
valid primary submissions. A reserve replaces only an invalid or missing primary submission from
the same form. Answers, agreement with gold, and confidence never affect replacement.

## Eligibility and exclusions

A valid submission must:

1. report age 18 or older;
2. confirm independent English reading;
3. provide consent;
4. report no generative AI, search, machine translation, or other-person assistance;
5. report no display or technical issue affecting comprehension;
6. complete the end check and all 12 referent, execution, and confidence responses.

Duplicate participant codes, wrong-form submissions, and unassigned codes are errors. Exclusions and
reserve use are retained in a selection ledger without personal identity.

## Estimands

Referent identity and execution choice are analyzed separately. Referent outcomes include
majority--gold agreement, unanimity, Fleiss' kappa, Krippendorff's nominal alpha, and complete-pair
majority accuracy. Execution outcomes include the same agreement statistics, the distribution of
entity/Reject/Clarify majorities, Clarify rate, and the count of items where referent identity agrees
with gold while execution choice does not.

All outcomes, including disagreement with author gold and fallback-policy disagreement, are kept.
The analysis script requires five labels for every item and all 18 changed pairs before producing a
report.

## Private inputs and release boundary

The private answer key, participant-to-platform response mapping, timestamps, and any payment
records remain outside the anonymous artifact. The artifact may contain de-identified item-level
labels, the frozen protocol, analysis code, tests, aggregate report, and hashes of private source
files when allowed by the study approval.
