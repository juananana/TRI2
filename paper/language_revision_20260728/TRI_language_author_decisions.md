# TRI Language Review: Author Decisions

The following items were not changed because each could affect claim strength, technical meaning,
or bibliographic facts.

| 位置 | 原文 | 问题 | 为什么不能自动修改 | 推荐选项 |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| Supplement, line 506 | `Rule* ... is statistically tied with GLM and DeepSeek CTA` | “Statistically tied” may overinterpret intervals that include zero. | Failure to detect a difference is not automatically evidence of equivalence. | If no equivalence test was run, use: `the reported intervals do not distinguish Rule* from GLM or DeepSeek CTA.` |
| Supplement, line 574 | `v3 AUC`, `Rewrite AUC` | AUC is not expanded, and the curve type is unspecified. | Expanding it to ROC AUC would add a technical fact not explicitly stated. | Confirm the implemented curve; then define `area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (ROC AUC)` or the correct alternative. |
| Supplement, line 810 | `the transfer loss is mainly selector grounding` | This may sound broader and more causal than the single Qwen 80-row oracle decomposition. | Changing it requires deciding the intended claim strength. | Candidate: `In this Qwen 80-row stress set, the oracle replacements localize most of the observed gap to selector grounding.` |
| Supplement, line 869 | `Mechanism-oriented labels` | “Mechanism” may conflict with the controller-level, non-latent claim boundary. | The authors may intend operational error mechanisms rather than model mechanisms. | Candidate: `Stage-oriented error labels used in write-consequence reports.` |
| Supplement, lines 1165--1166 | `Qwen's statistically indistinguishable comparison` | This wording may imply an equivalence conclusion. | The reported interval including zero does not by itself establish indistinguishability. | Candidate: `For Qwen, the reported interval includes zero, so this comparison does not establish ...` |
| Supplement, lines 1332--1333 | `demonstrates that ... can be instantiated` | `demonstrates` may be stronger than needed for an exploratory pilot. | The current sentence is arguably defensible, but the desired evidentiary tone is an author choice. | Candidate: `shows one stateful external-style tool-loop instantiation of the diagnosis.` |
| Supplement, lines 1546--1547 | `This is a reproducible pre-binding temporal-order failure` | “Reproducible” may imply an independent replication rather than recurrence in two app cases. | The intended meaning of reproducibility is unclear. | Candidate: `The same pre-binding temporal-order failure appears in both app cases.` |
| Supplement, lines 1605--1606 | `This rules out dominance by one domain or template family` | “Rules out” is stronger than a leave-one-group-out sensitivity result. | Rephrasing changes inferential strength. | Candidate: `No single domain or template-family exclusion reverses the observed direction.` |
| Supplement, lines 1628--1630 | `the method direction and conditional mechanism are stable` | “Conditional mechanism” may imply mechanism identification. | The paper otherwise limits itself to behavioral/controller-level evidence. | Candidate: `the method direction and conditional substitution pattern are stable across these repeats.` |
| Supplement, section title and lines 1783--1786 | `Independent Human-Rewrite Model Evaluation`; `one independent volunteer` | “Independent” can be read as independent elicitation evidence, which the paper explicitly does not claim. | It may instead mean only “uninvolved in TRI design.” | Prefer `Human-Rewrite Model Evaluation` and `one volunteer uninvolved in TRI design`, unless a stronger documented independence definition is intended. |
